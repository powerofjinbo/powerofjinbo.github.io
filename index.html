About

I am Jinbo Zhang, a graduate student and researcher at the University of California, Irvine. My work focuses on particle physics, machine learning methods for high-energy physics, and photonics materials.

My recent projects include convolutional neural network systems for strong-lensing discovery, experimental studies of epsilon-near-zero photonics, and collider phenomenology. I am interested in data-driven discovery, reproducible analysis, and building open research pipelines.

Curriculum Vitae
"I care most about research that can move both theory and practice forward with clear evidence and transparent methods."
